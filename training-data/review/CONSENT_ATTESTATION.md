# First-party Twi consent attestation

Date recorded: 2026-09-27

Scope: the first-party Twi recordings listed in `datasets/team_recordings_inventory.csv` and the duplicate source `training-data/21.opus`.

The project owner has attested that the team owns these recordings and that the required speaker consent for model training and evaluation has been obtained.

This repository record captures the project-level attestation only. It does **not** replace or reproduce the underlying consent artifacts; those should remain retained in the organization's controlled records.
